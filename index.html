ABOUT US — EST. 30 JULY 2025

Truth in Every Story

Independent Malawian digital television under NADA Tech Studios.

WiMuSS TV is a Malawian digital television and news media platform based in Lilongwe, operating under NADA Tech Studios. Founded on 30 July 2025 by tech entrepreneur Edgar Mphepo, who also works as a journalist with WiMuSS TV, we began with viral studio interviews and school excellence coverage. We report in English and Chichewa.

Mission

Accurate, fearless journalism that informs and empowers Malawians.
