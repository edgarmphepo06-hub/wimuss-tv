About Us • Est. 30 July 2025

Truth in Every Story

An independent Malawian digital television and news media platform.

WiMuSS TV is a Malawian digital television and news media platform based in Lilongwe, operating under NADA Tech Studios. Founded on 30 July 2025 by tech entrepreneur Edgar Mphepo, who also works as a journalist with WiMuSS TV, we began with viral studio interviews and school excellence coverage and have grown into a full news desk reporting in English and Chichewa.

Our journalists follow a simple newsroom rule: verify first, publish second. Every bulletin, interview and feature is checked against named sources before it reaches our audience, because credibility is the only currency that matters in journalism.

Our Mission

To practise accurate, fearless and independent journalism that informs, inspires and empowers Malawians — on television and online.

Our Vision

To become Malawi's most trusted digital television and news media platform, delivering truth in every story, on every screen.
