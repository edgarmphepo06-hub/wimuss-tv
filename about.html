Our Story

WiMuSS TV is a Malawian digital television and news media platform based in Lilongwe, operating under NADA Tech Studios. Founded on 30 July 2025 by tech entrepreneur Edgar Mphepo, who also works as a journalist with WiMuSS TV, we began with viral studio interviews and school excellence coverage and have grown into a trusted national voice — covering politics, business, sports, culture and community life from the Warm Heart of Africa to the world.

We report in English and Chichewa so every Malawian can access verified, impartial news.

Our Mission

To deliver accurate, fearless and people-centred journalism that informs, educates and empowers Malawians.

Our Vision

To be Malawi's most trusted digital television platform — truth in every story, on every screen.
